**Description**

```
Start with a bakery. Build a holding.

CapitalForge Lite is a business game where you build the supply chain yourself. Every chain has three stages: raw materials, processing and retail. The slowest link slows the whole chain; find the bottleneck, grow it, multiply your profit.

BUILD YOUR CHAINS
• Bakery, coffee, fashion, leather and tech; then the energy, auto and aviation islands.
• Produce your own raw materials, process them and sell them in your own shops: an integrated chain earns the best margin.
• Milestones double your speed, and buildings rise from shops to towers to landmarks.

OUTSELL YOUR RIVAL
• Your rival chases your sales; lose market share and your income drops.
• Run campaigns and turn city events and council decisions in your favour.
• Tap the deal bubbles that pop up over the city: bulk orders, celebrity customers, investors.

GROW YOUR HOLDING
• Take your company public and watch the share price climb.
• Sell the city: every sale leaves permanent stars and a trade route in the next city.
• Coastal, mountain, desert and snowy cities; every city you've sold on the holding map.

COMPETE
• Race in a group of 30 in the weekly league: the top five move up.
• Start a private league with friends using an invite code.
• Show your stars on the Game Center leaderboard.

You keep earning while you're away, longer with a General Manager. No ads, no tracking. On iPhone and iPad, in light and dark.
```

## İnceleme notları (App Review Information)

```
CapitalForge Lite is an idle business game. No login is required to play.

The weekly league is optional: tap the league card ("Haftalık lig"), choose a company name and join. Accounts are anonymous (no email or password). Scores are only accepted from genuine devices via Apple App Attest, so the league cannot submit scores from the Simulator. Users can delete their league account in the app: League → Delete account ("Hesabı sil").

Game Center is used for a global stars leaderboard (cf.lite.stars).

The only user-generated content visible to others is company and league names; they pass a profanity filter. There is no chat.

All notifications are local.
```

İletişim bilgisi: ad, soyad, telefon ve e-posta inceleme ekibi için (herkese görünmüyor).
