## English

**Short description** (243)

```
Build a company in a living city: find empty plots, grow your own supply chain and take over your rivals on the stock market. Energy, logistics, real estate, seasons and rivals who remember — in a free city, ten scenarios or the weekly league.
```

**About this game**

```
Most of the city is already built. You will grow by finding the empty plots — or by buying someone else's business.

[h2]Read the city[/h2]
[list]
[*]Lenses show demand, opportunity, land value and competition on the map.
[*]Nine kinds of district, seven categories: grocery, dining, retail, electronics, services, housing, office.
[*]Every investment shows its estimate up front: what it costs, what it earns, how many days until it pays back.
[/list]

[h2]Build your chain[/h2]
[list]
[*]40 buildings, 25 goods. From coffee plantation to cafe, from cotton field to boutique.
[*]The chain card shows the bottleneck: which link you own, which you buy from the market, what lifts your margin most.
[*]A freight terminal and haulage contracts; power plants, the energy price and the energy crisis; housing and office projects, rent, occupancy and urban renewal.
[/list]

[h2]Beat your rivals[/h2]
[list]
[*]Eight rival profiles, each with a CEO, a temperament and a memory: they remember alliances and grudges alike.
[*]Gather shares on the stock market, go public, take over a rival; defend your own shares against a raid and break up the consortium formed against you.
[*]Lobby on council motions, bid in land auctions, sponsor city projects.
[/list]

[h2]A city that lives[/h2]
[list]
[*]Crises, eras and decision cards; trends in categories, fads in goods.
[*]Four seasons and weather: snow, rain, fog; traffic by day, lit windows at night.
[*]Unions, credit ratings, a monthly quest board and deals that pop up in the city.
[/list]

[h2]Choose your way[/h2]
[list]
[*]Free city: Relaxed, Balanced or Ruthless; four extra rules make it harder still. Found a holding and run three cities at once.
[*]Ten scenarios with three stars each, from Rescue to Urban Renewal.
[*]The weekly Seed League: everyone in the same city, under the same rules. The daily run, the rule of the week, the leader's ghost curve and invite-only friend leagues.
[*]A career with 29 badges; every badge is a Steam achievement.
[/list]

[h2]Make your own[/h2]
[list]
[*]Scenario editor: set the city, the rules, the goals and the stars yourself; share it as a code or a file.
[*]Your run in one picture: save and share the city card.
[/list]

[h2]On your computer[/h2]
[list]
[*]Saves are files and the game plays without a connection; the league needs the internet.
[*]English, Turkish and German.
[*]Text size, a colour-blind friendly palette, reduced motion, separate volume levels and full keyboard use.
[/list]
```

## Sistem gereksinimleri

Oyun bir web görünümünde çalışıyor (Tauri): Windows'ta Microsoft Edge
WebView2, macOS'ta WebKit, Linux'ta WebKitGTK. Şehir WebGL 2 ile çiziliyor.
Değerler tahmin: oyun orta sınıf telefonda da akıcı (Tur 65), alt sınır
bu yüzden düşük; eski bir bilgisayarda denenmedi. Steamworks'e İngilizce
giriliyor.

| | En az (Minimum) | Önerilen (Recommended) |
|---|---|---|
| Windows | Windows 10 64-bit, Microsoft Edge WebView2 Runtime | Windows 11 |
| macOS | macOS 12 Monterey, Apple Silicon ya da Intel | macOS 14 Sonoma |
| Linux / SteamOS | Ubuntu 22.04 ya da eşdeğeri, WebKitGTK 4.1 (`libwebkit2gtk-4.1-0`) | Ubuntu 24.04 |
| İşlemci | 2 GHz, iki çekirdek | 4 çekirdek |
| Bellek | 4 GB RAM | 8 GB RAM |
| Ekran kartı | WebGL 2 destekleyen herhangi bir kart (Intel HD 5000 ya da üstü) | Intel Iris Xe, GeForce GTX 1050, Radeon RX 560 |
| Disk | 200 MB | 200 MB |
| Ağ | Lig için internet bağlantısı | — |

## Yapay zekâ beyanı

Steamworks'ün içerik anketi (Content Survey) oyunun geliştirilmesinde
yapay zekâ kullanıldıysa bunu soruyor ve mağaza sayfasında gösteriyor.
CapitalForge'un kodu, metinleri ve çevirileri (Almanca dahil) yapay zekâ
destekli bir kodlama yardımcısıyla yazıldı; şehir grafikleri ve müzik
koddan üretiliyor (prosedürel), hazır görsel ya da ses kullanılmadı.
Oyun çalışırken yapay zekâ içerik üretmiyor. Anketteki karar ve son
metin yayıncının; önerilen İngilizce metin:

```
The game's code, in-game text and translations were written with the help of AI tools. City graphics and music are generated procedurally by the game's own code. The game does not generate any content with AI while you play.
```
